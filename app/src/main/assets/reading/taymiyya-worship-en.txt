Short quotation from the Arabic original

« العبادة هي اسم جامع لكل ما يحبه الله ويرضاه من الأقوال والأعمال الباطنة والظاهرة »

Translation prepared for Selkie

“Worship is a comprehensive term for everything God loves and approves of, among inward and outward words and deeds.”

Understanding this passage — commentary written for Selkie

Here Ibn Taymiyya gives a religious definition connecting visible actions with inner life. The passage invites reflection on both what we do and the meaning we give our actions.

Putting it into practice — advice written for Selkie

Before a useful action, take a moment to clarify your intention: what good would you like to contribute? Then choose one small, concrete step and carry it out attentively. You can write your reflection on paper.
