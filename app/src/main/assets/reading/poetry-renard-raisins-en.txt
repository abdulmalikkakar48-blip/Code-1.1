English translation prepared for Selkie — complete poem, free verse

A Gascon fox, others say a Norman,
Almost dying of hunger, saw high on a vine
Grapes that seemed ripe,
Covered in a reddish skin.
The fellow would gladly have made a meal of them;
But as he could not reach them:
They are too green, he said, and fit for boors.

Was that not better than complaining?
