English prose translation prepared for Selkie

Help others whenever you can: we often need someone smaller than ourselves. Two fables will show this truth, for evidence is plentiful.

A rat came out between a lion’s paws, rather carelessly. On this occasion the king of the animals showed his greatness and spared its life. This kindness was not wasted. Who would have believed that a lion would need a rat?

Yet, on leaving the forest, the lion was caught in a net. Its roars could not free it. The rat hurried over and worked with its teeth until one gnawed strand broke the whole trap.

Patience and time achieve more than strength or rage.

Putting it into practice — advice written for Selkie
When a task seems difficult, choose one small action you can repeat patiently. Think also of someone you could help in a simple way.
