English translation prepared for Selkie — complete poem, free verse

Work; make an effort:
That is the resource least likely to fail.

A rich farmer, sensing his death was near,
Called his children and spoke to them alone.
Take care, he said, not to sell the inheritance
Our parents left to us:
A treasure is hidden there.
I do not know where, but a little courage
Will help you find it; you will manage it.
Turn over your field once the harvest is in:
Dig, search, use your spades; leave no spot
Where your hands do not pass and pass again.
After their father's death, the sons turned over the field,
Here, there, everywhere; so well that by the end of the year
It yielded more than before.
No money was hidden there. But the father was wise
To show them, before his death,
That work is a treasure.
