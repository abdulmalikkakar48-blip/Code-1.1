Fragment of a list in the Arabic original — cuts indicated

« … وصدق الحديث وأداء الأمانة وبر الوالدين وصلة الأرحام والوفاء بالعهود … »

Translation prepared for Selkie

“… speaking truthfully, honouring what is entrusted to us, being kind to parents, maintaining family ties and keeping commitments …”

Understanding this passage — commentary written for Selkie

In this fragment, Ibn Taymiyya includes ordinary actions in his discussion of worship. The passage highlights reliability in words and commitments. It does not reproduce the complete list or the complete work.

Putting it into practice — advice written for Selkie

Choose one simple commitment you can realistically keep today: return something entrusted to you or finish a small task you promised. At the end of the day, consider what you did and what you can improve tomorrow.
