English translation prepared for Selkie — complete poem, free verse

In the endless
Weariness of the plain,
The uncertain snow
Gleams like sand.

The sky is copper,
Without any glow;
One might think one sees
The moon live and die.

Like clouds,
The grey oaks float,
Of the nearby forests,
Among the mists.

The sky is copper,
Without any glow.
One might think one sees
The moon live and die.

Wheezing crow,
And you, lean wolves,
In these biting winds,
What is happening to you?

In the endless
Weariness of the plain,
The uncertain snow
Gleams like sand.
