Selkie privacy policy — test version 0.22.0

Information updated on 17 October 2026, for version 0.22.0 (versionCode 35). This policy describes local tracking, the Coach, calendar, summaries, favourites, limit time slots, widget, programmes, Selkie Dice and colouring pages. Accounts, payments and remote AI dialogue are not enabled.

Selkie helps you reduce your screen time. In this free test version, tracking data stays on your device and tracking does not require an account. The local Coach uses prepared advice, without an account or sending data over the Internet. You can read this information without granting tracking access.

The policy is available offline, before permissions are granted and from the ☰ menu → Privacy and data. Reading it does not start tracking, and no mandatory acceptance is required. Terms of use, subscription and refund information, and publisher information are accessible from the same page. French and English present the same information.

What data does Selkie use?

Selkie checks the names, icons and identifiers of apps accessible from your launcher locally, so you can choose which apps to track.

After you grant usage access, Selkie reads Android events from midnight in your local time zone to calculate how long you use tracked apps and check whether a blocked app is in the foreground. The foreground app identifier may also refer to an app you do not track: it is used temporarily in memory, without Selkie saving a history of it.

Selkie stores the following on your device: the identifiers of selected apps, their limits and time slots, the reference usage durations used for resets, lock, alert and penalty dates, points and the latest Points-mode dice receipt, completed challenges and local dates of daily check-ins, the identifiers, creation dates, titles and three steps of personal challenges, their duration and daily checkboxes, the selected demonstration plan and progress cycle dates. This also includes structured history of observed usage durations and breaches and check-ins, references or advice selected as favourites with their original language and minimum plan, and the identifiers of selected programmes with their start and manual check-in dates. Android keeps its own usage statistics independently of Selkie.

To measure observed intervals, Selkie keeps a last technical sample of raw Android usage durations, with its date and time zone, to compare consecutive observations no more than 30 seconds apart. It is replaced by the next sample or discarded during a pause or cleanup; it is not exported. History applies technical caps of 500 app identifiers in daily records and 64 observed check-in identifiers per day. These history storage limits are separate from the plans and do not add a commercial cap to the number of apps tracked with Ultra.

Selkie does not read messages from your other apps, screen content, contacts, photos, location or your phone’s calendar. Selkie’s calendar shows its own local progress; it requests no permission to access the phone’s calendar. There is no tracking synchronisation, advertising, audience analytics or active payment service.

The local Coach chooses advice and answers about Selkie from a prepared catalogue. Your messages and, when needed for a particular tip, your points and tracked app usage durations are used only on your phone. This coach is not an AI model and cannot answer every new question.

Conversations stay in memory while the Coach tab is open. Saving messages the Coach did not understand is an option that is off by default. If you enable it, up to fifty distinct messages of no more than eight hundred characters each remain in the phone’s private storage. No message is automatically sent to the publisher. Messages recognised as sensitive or related to a crisis are not saved; this detection may be imperfect, so avoid entering private information.

Your French/English language preference and light, night or phone-controlled appearance choice are also stored only on your device. Changing language or appearance sends no data to a server.

Calendar, summaries, favourites and plan features

The Free plan includes the progress calendar and weekly summary. Their history covers at most the last 90 rolling local days. Usage history concerns only selected apps and intervals of active tracking that were actually observed; pauses and gaps without observations are excluded. An actually observed active interval can record zero usage, including when no app is selected; this does not fill gaps without observations. No earlier history is invented or added retroactively. Days without observations are unknown, not zero-usage days; only observed days can enter an average. “Observed breaches” do not establish that a whole day was observed or that a day without a breach was perfect. This history is separate from the current daily counter, which continues to use Android events and tracking reference durations.

Favourites are available on the Free plan, with a limit of 50 tips you select from Selkie’s authored or prepared advice. They retain the prepared tip reference or selected authored reply, its original language and minimum plan, rather than your raw input, an unrecognised message or sensitive text. Paid answers to prepared tips are not stored in favourites or copied into the export. Favourites do not copy the optional unrecognised-message log. After a downgrade, the content of a favourite requiring a paid plan remains locked until a compatible plan is selected.

Premium and Ultra add a weekly breakdown by app and up to 20 limit time slots in total for selected apps, with days and times. At zero points, you cannot create, edit, enable or delete a time slot. A time slot’s applicable limit uses the current counter: changing slots does not reset that counter. A 24-hour lock that has already started continues until it expires, even after the slot ends or a downgrade to Free. On that plan, rules remain stored but paid time slots no longer apply.

The Premium widget, also available with Ultra, displays points and a suggested challenge on the phone’s home screen. Anyone with access to that screen may see this information. To add it: long-press the home screen → Widgets → Selkie. It uses local data and creates no account or network transmission.

Ultra offers programmes you choose: Sleep for 7 days, Studies for 14 days and Social media for 30 days. Check-ins are manual, at most once per local day across all programmes, with no extra points. Selkie stores the programme identifier and dates needed for its progress; it does not automatically verify the activity you report. A downgrade retains this progress but locks it until Ultra is selected again.

Free Selkie Dice and colouring

The three Selkie Dice break modes suggest an activity, a break from a selected app or break days within the next seven days. Their unconfirmed choices and results stay temporarily in memory, without a saved roll history. Confirming only adds personal calendar tasks with their text, date and completion state. Check-ins are manual, with no extra points, automatic blocking or changes to existing limits. These tasks remain until you delete them, reset all data or uninstall the app; they are included in your chosen export.

Honour points are capped at 100: older balances above 100 are reduced to 100, lower balances are not increased, and the usual rewards remain available up to that cap. The fourth dice mode, Points, is free and optional. Its cost is explained before rolling: it subtracts 1 to 6 points according to the face rolled, without exceeding the available balance or going below zero. You cannot start a new roll at zero points. This mode offers no winnings, prizes, money or paid credits; points are not sold. Any 24-hour reward already due is applied before the deduction.

To recover the result after an interruption and prevent a second deduction for the same roll, Selkie keeps only the latest Points-mode receipt: operation identifier, face rolled, points deducted and resulting balance. The next roll replaces this receipt; resetting all data or uninstalling the app deletes it. There is no log of all rolls. This receipt stays in private phone storage and is included in the export you request, without sending it to the publisher.

Colouring is free on every plan. Illustrations offers twenty original drawings: eight in Flowers, seven in Landscapes and five in Patterns. The eight earlier illustrations and their saved colours are preserved. To colour, tap a closed region to fill it using sixteen colours, an eraser, zoom and optional numbered guides. Under Grid pages, the twelve 12 × 12-cell pages and their ten-colour palette remain available: eight geometric patterns and four small drawings, a tree, a flower, an open book and a landscape. Colours already saved for those pages are preserved. Selkie keeps only each page identifier and the colours of its regions or cells in private storage on this phone. Undo and redo retain at most fifty gestures in memory while the page is open, without a saved history. Each page remains until you reset it, erase all data or uninstall the app. These built-in drawings are original; no images are downloaded and no drawings are sent to a server. No access to photos or files is requested. Saved colours from both formats are included in your chosen JSON export; exported copies must be deleted separately.

Why is data used, and on what basis?

This data is used only to provide the service you request: setting limits and time slots, measuring usage, displaying alerts and blocking screens, presenting progress in the calendar, summaries and widget, finding favourite tips, following selected programmes, planning confirmed breaks, applying the voluntary points deduction and recovering its latest result without a duplicate deduction, and resuming colouring pages. The legal basis for processing necessary to provide this service is performance of the service at your request, under Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR.

Android permissions control technical access to phone features. They do not replace a legal basis under the GDPR and do not count as consent to advertising or data sharing. Reading this policy does not require you to accept it and does not start tracking.

Optional saving of messages the Coach did not understand lets you find them again on your device and is based on your freely given, specific, informed and withdrawable consent under Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR. You can withdraw that choice at any time: turning the option off deletes messages already saved. Declining to save them does not prevent you from using the Coach, limits or challenges.

What are permissions for?

Usage access: calculating usage durations and identifying the foreground app. Display over other apps: showing the blocking screen when a limit is reached. Both accesses are required for tracking with blocking; without them, this feature does not start.

Notifications: warning you before a limit and making tracking visible. Alerts may show the name of a tracked app; you can change their lock-screen visibility in Android settings. You can decline notifications, and doing so does not grant any other access.

The foreground service continues local tracking and may restart after the phone restarts if the required accesses remain granted. You can revoke each permission in Android settings; revoking a required access stops tracking with blocking.

Who receives the data?

Tracking data and local Coach conversations are not sent to a server or to the publisher. The local Coach does not require an account, make network requests or automatically export saved messages. Opening the app or the Coach sends no message.

Android backup is disabled for Selkie. However, migration between devices may depend on the manufacturer and its tools; this policy does not guarantee how they work. Processing by Android, the manufacturer or the download platform is covered by their own privacy information.

If you email us, your address and message are transmitted through your email service and received at the contact address, together with technical information associated with the email. The publisher and their email provider then process that message. Do not include sensitive data or screenshots containing private information.

Requests to exercise your rights are processed to meet GDPR obligations under Article 6(1)(c). Other questions are processed on the basis of the legitimate interest in replying to people who contact the publisher, under Article 6(1)(f); you may object. Messages are kept while replying, then deleted, unless a legal obligation requires further retention.

The contact address uses Gmail. The controller must still check and specify email hosting arrangements and any transfers outside the European Economic Area before public distribution. Selkie does not host or transfer your local tracking data.

How long is data kept?

Settings and progress remain on your device until you delete them from Selkie or uninstall it, subject to the limited history retention period. Removing an app from tracking deletes its entry, limit and lock; points and challenge totals remain until a full data reset. Observations already recorded for the calendar and summaries, including check-ins, remain after this removal. Only observations from the last 90 rolling local days appear in the history and its export. Cleaning older entries from storage resumes on the next app use, on a new day or during an observation; it depends on the app running and the write succeeding. Timer-based deletion while the app is not running is not guaranteed. Current-counter usage durations are recalculated from Android events; they are separate from this observed history.

This data is kept in the app’s private storage. Deleting data removes Selkie’s local data, including tracked apps, locks, points, the selected demonstration plan, personal challenges with their steps and dates, calendar and summary history and its last technical sample, personal calendar tasks including confirmed dice breaks, the latest Points-mode receipt, saved illustrations and grid colouring pages, time slots, favourites, programmes and their dates, and saved unrecognised Coach messages and their saving preference. The widget returns to a neutral display without previous progress. On your next use, progress starts again from the initial values. Selkie alerts are removed. If deletion cannot be confirmed, a message asks you to try again. This action does not delete Android statistics or automatically revoke permissions granted in the phone’s settings. It also clears your language preference: Selkie returns to English if the phone’s language is English, and to French otherwise. Appearance returns to Light.

Check-in dates used by Selkie’s built-in challenge cycle are deleted when it renews, every 21 days. Check-ins already recorded in the calendar history follow its rolling retention period of 90 local days. The total number of completed challenges and points remain until a full data reset. Personal challenges and their dates remain until you delete them individually or reset all data; they are not reset when the cycle changes. Favourites and time slots remain until you delete them or reset all data; a plan change does not grant access to paid features. A programme’s progress remains until you reset it, remove it or reset all data, with at most 7, 14 or 30 check-in dates depending on the programme. The last check-in date shared across programmes remains stored when switching, resetting or removing a programme to enforce the once-per-day limit; a full data reset removes it too.

The local Coach conversation stays only in memory while its tab is open. Unrecognised messages saved through the optional setting remain until you delete them, turn saving off, reset all data or uninstall the app. Beyond fifty messages, the oldest are removed. Deleting only the messages keeps your saving preference for future questions; a full data reset turns it off.

Deleting a personal challenge does not remove points already earned. Successful deletion is announced only after deletion writes are confirmed. Exported copies must be deleted separately by the user: deleting app data does not remove them.

This native version uses no advertising cookies, advertising SDK, audience analytics or advertising tracking identifier. No non-essential cookies or trackers are used by this version’s native features. Local preferences support the features you request; optional saving of unrecognised questions is separate and off by default. No cookie banner is added for these local features alone.

The offline test version does not declare Internet permission. Links you choose to open, your email app and the file provider you select are separate apps or services with their own privacy information. Adding trackers or an online service would require a new assessment and, where required, prior consent.

Export your settings and progress

Export my data prepares a local JSON file containing stored settings and challenges, structured history still retained from the last 90 local days, time-slot rules, personal calendar tasks including confirmed dice breaks, the latest valid Points-mode receipt if present, saved illustrations and grid colouring pages, favourite references or tips and their metadata, and programme identifiers and dates. Only fields defined for this export are copied. You choose where to save it using Android. The last raw technical sample of Android usage durations, unconfirmed Activity, App and Week dice results, raw Coach messages including unrecognised messages, sign-in tokens and email addresses are excluded. No file is automatically sent to the publisher.

The JSON file uses format version 6. It includes the 100-point cap, the latest valid Points-mode receipt if present, saved colours for illustrations and grid pages, and personal tasks including confirmed dice breaks. Unconfirmed results from the three break modes and colouring undo history are not included.

The file may contain private information, including tracked apps and your challenge text. If you choose an online storage provider, it may synchronise the file under its own rules. An exported copy remains in your chosen location after resetting or uninstalling Selkie; delete it separately if you wish.

Your rights and choices

You can view and change your limits, remove an app and delete Selkie data from the app or Android settings. You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and, where the conditions are met, portability of your data. The right to object applies to processing that provides for it; the necessary service is based on performance at your request. Use the contact above for any question or request about these rights.

The publisher does not receive your local tracking data or Coach messages and therefore cannot view or delete them remotely. Do not send them unsolicited to exercise your rights. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority.

Blocking and points are personal screen-time aids: they are not decisions producing legal effects or similarly significant effects within the meaning of Article 22 of the GDPR.

Requests about your rights normally receive a response within one month of receipt. Complex or numerous requests may require a two-month extension; you must be told the reason during the first month. Do not send identity documents unsolicited: proportionate verification may be requested only where there is reasonable doubt about the requester’s identity.
